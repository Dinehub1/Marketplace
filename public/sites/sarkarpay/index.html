Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SarkarPay.

You can start accepting payments in under 5 minutes! Simply sign up, complete your KYC (which takes less than 2 minutes), and integrate our payment button or API. For no-code solutions, just create a payment link and share it with your customers.

We support all major payment methods: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and 500+ apps), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking (50+ banks), wallets, and EMI options. We also support international cards for cross-border payments.

Our Starter plan has 2.0% per transaction with no monthly fee. Growth plan is ₹499/month + 1.5% per transaction. Enterprise plan is ₹1,999/month + 1.0% per transaction with volume discounts available. There are no hidden charges, setup fees, or annual maintenance costs.

Unlike traditional payment gateways that settle in T+1 or T+2 days, SarkarPay settles your payments instantly to your registered bank account. This works 24/7 including weekends and bank holidays. You can also schedule settlements at your preferred time.

Absolutely. We are PCI DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of payment security certification. All data is encrypted with 256-bit SSL. We support 3D Secure 2.0 for card payments and have AI-powered fraud detection that monitors every transaction in real-time. We never store sensitive card data on our servers.
