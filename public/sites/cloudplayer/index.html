FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about Cloud Player.

How does zero buffering work?
Our cloud-native architecture uses edge servers located close to you. Content is pre-cached and delivered via adaptive bitrate streaming, which automatically adjusts quality based on your connection speed for instant, stutter-free playback.
Can I watch on multiple devices?
Yes! Premium plan supports 3 simultaneous devices, Family plan supports 5. Your watch history, preferences, and library sync seamlessly across all your devices.
Is 4K HDR available on all plans?
4K HDR and Dolby Vision are available on Premium and Family plans. Basic plan streams in 720p HD. All plans support adaptive quality based on your internet speed.
How do offline downloads work?
Premium and Family plans allow you to download content for offline viewing. Downloads are stored securely on your device and available for 30 days. Perfect for flights and commutes!
What Smart TVs are supported?
Cloud Player works on Android TV, Amazon Fire Stick, Samsung Tizen TV, LG WebOS, Apple TV, and Chromecast. Essentially any device that supports modern streaming apps.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely! No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your account settings and continue using until the end of your billing period.
