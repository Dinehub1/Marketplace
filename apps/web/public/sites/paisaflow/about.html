Our Story

From a Dorm Room Idea to 10 Million+ Users

PaisaFlow was born in 2022 when our founders, Arjun Mehta and Sneha Reddy, were frustrated by the clunky digital payment experiences available in India. Transfers failed, apps crashed, and customer support was nowhere to be found.

They envisioned a payment platform that just works — one that's fast enough for a street vendor in Delhi, simple enough for a grandmother in Chennai, and powerful enough for a startup in Bangalore.

Today, PaisaFlow processes over ₹500 crores in transactions every month. We serve 10 million+ users across all 28 states and 8 union territories. But we're just getting started.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From a two-person startup to India's fastest-growing payment platform.

January 2022

The Idea is Born

Arjun and Sneha sketch the first PaisaFlow prototype on a whiteboard at a Bangalore café. The vision: payments so simple, anyone can use them.

August 2022

Seed Funding Secured

Raised ₹5 crores from leading Indian angel investors. The team grows to 8 engineers working out of a small Koramangala office.

March 2023

Public Launch

PaisaFlow goes live on Android and iOS. 50,000 users sign up in the first week. App Store rating: 4.8 stars.

November 2023

1 Million Users

Cross the 1 million user milestone. Launch merchant QR payments and business accounts for small shop owners.

June 2024

Series A Funding

Raised ₹50 crores Series A from Sequoia India. Expanded to 15 Indian languages and launched USSD support for feature phones.

December 2024

10 Million Users

Hit 10 million monthly active users. Processing ₹500+ crores in monthly transactions. Team grows to 120+ across 3 offices.

2026 & Beyond

The Road Ahead

AI-powered financial insights, international remittances, and a full-stack nebbank license. The mission continues — every Indian deserves seamless digital payments.
