FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

SarkarConnect is an authorized facilitation platform that helps citizens access government services more easily. We are partnered with various government departments and CSC (Common Service Centres) to provide a seamless digital experience.

We use bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and follow ISO 27001 security standards. Your data is stored in India-based servers and we never share your information with third parties without consent. We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We accept all major payment methods including UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking from 50+ banks, and wallets. All transactions are processed through RBI-compliant payment gateways.

Processing times vary by service. Simple services like bill payments are instant. Document applications typically take 3-15 working days depending on the department. Pro and Enterprise users get priority processing which can reduce wait times by up to 50%.

Yes! Every application gets a unique tracking ID. You can check status anytime from your dashboard or via SMS/email notifications. We update status in real-time as your application moves through various stages of processing.

If your application is rejected, we provide detailed reasons and guidance on how to fix the issues. Pro and Enterprise users get free re-submission assistance. Our support team will help you correct and resubmit your application at no extra charge.

Yes! Our mobile app is available for both Android (Play Store) and iOS (App Store). The app includes all web features plus offline form filling, document scanning with OCR, and push notifications for status updates.

Government fees are non-refundable as per department policies. However, our service fee is fully refundable if we are unable to process your application due to a platform error. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
