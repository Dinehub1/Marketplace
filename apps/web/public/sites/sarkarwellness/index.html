Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Ayurvedic treatments and our services.

Ayurveda is a 5,000-year-old system of natural healing that originated in India. It works on the principle of balancing three fundamental energies or doshas — Vata (air), Pitta (fire), and Kapha (earth). When these doshas are in balance, the body is healthy. Ayurveda uses herbal medicines, diet, yoga, massage, and lifestyle changes to restore this balance and promote natural healing.

Yes, Panchakarma is completely safe when performed by qualified Ayurvedic practitioners. It's a gentle yet powerful detoxification process. You can expect a series of treatments over 7-21 days including oil massages, steam therapy, herbal enemas, and nasal cleansing. Most patients feel deeply relaxed and rejuvenated after the process. Our Vaidyas customize the intensity based on your constitution and health condition.

Results vary depending on the condition, its severity, and your commitment to the treatment plan. Many patients report feeling improvements in energy, sleep, and digestion within 2-4 weeks. Chronic conditions may require 3-6 months of consistent treatment. Ayurveda focuses on root-cause healing rather than quick fixes, so the results are sustainable and long-lasting.

All our herbal medicines are prepared using traditional formulations from classical Ayurvedic texts. They are 100% natural, made from herbs, minerals, and plant extracts.
