Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SarkarMart.

SarkarMart supports multiple payment methods including UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), credit/debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery. UPI payments are instant and secure. Simply select your preferred payment method at checkout.

We offer a 7-day easy return policy on most products. Go to your Orders page, select the item you want to return, choose a reason, and schedule a free pickup. Your refund will be processed within 24-48 hours of receiving the returned item.

Delivery times vary by plan and location. Free members get standard delivery in 5-7 business days. Prime members get next-day delivery across 500+ cities. Business members get same-day delivery in select metro cities. You can track your order in real-time from the Orders page.

SarkarMart delivers to 10,000+ pin codes across India. We cover all major cities, tier-2 and tier-3 towns. Enter your pin code on any product page to check delivery availability and estimated delivery date for your location.

You can cancel your order before it is shipped. Go to Orders → Select the order → Click "Cancel Order". If the order is already shipped, you can refuse delivery or initiate a return after receiving it. The refund will be processed to your original payment method.

SarkarMart Prime is our premium membership at just ₹99/month. Benefits include unlimited free delivery, next-day delivery, early access to deals and sales, exclusive Prime-only discounts, and priority customer support. Try it free for 30 days!
