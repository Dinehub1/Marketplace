Our Story

We're on a mission to digitize every local business in India — one shop at a time.

From a Small Idea to 50,000+ Businesses

SarkarDukaan was born in 2023 from a simple observation: millions of local businesses in India had no online presence. Customers couldn't find them, and they couldn't reach new customers beyond their neighborhood.

Our founders, Rajesh Sharma and Priya Patel, experienced this firsthand when they tried to find a reliable electrician in their city. The search was frustrating — scattered information, no reviews, no way to compare.

That frustration became our mission. Today, SarkarDukaan connects over 50,000 businesses with millions of customers across 50+ cities in India. We've helped local shops increase their revenue by an average of 35%.

But we're just getting started. Our goal? 1 million businesses by 2027.
