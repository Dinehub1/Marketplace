Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our service.

What internet speed do I need for 4K streaming?

▾

For optimal 4K HDR streaming, we recommend a minimum internet speed of 25 Mbps. However, our adaptive bitrate technology will adjust the quality based on your connection to ensure the best possible experience without buffering.

Can I share my account with family members?

▾

Yes! Depending on your plan, you can create multiple user profiles. The Basic plan allows 1 profile, Premium allows up to 4, and Family allows up to 6 simultaneous profiles with personalized recommendations and viewing histories.

Is there a free trial available?

▾

We offer a 7-day free trial for all new subscribers. You can explore all Premium features during the trial period, and if you decide not to continue, you can cancel anytime before the trial ends without any charges.

What devices are compatible with Cloud Player?

▾

Cloud Player is compatible with a wide range of devices including iOS and Android smartphones/tablets, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Android TV, Samsung Smart TVs, LG Smart TVs, PlayStation, Xbox, and web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

How does the offline download feature work?

▾

With our offline download feature, you can download movies and TV shows to watch later without an internet connection. Downloads are available for Premium and Family plans, and the number of simultaneous downloads depends on your subscription tier.

Is my data secure with Cloud Player?

▾

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. We also comply with GDPR and CCPA regulations to ensure your personal information is protected and handled with the utmost care.
