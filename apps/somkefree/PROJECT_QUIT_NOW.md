# 🚭 **Quit Now - Smoking Cessation App**

## 📌 Project Overview

**Quit Now** is a behavioral health mobile app designed to support users in their journey to quit smoking. Through visualized health data, gamified challenges, and motivational milestones, it encourages long-term commitment to a smoke-free life. The app blends emotional design with real-time analytics to make quitting both empowering and engaging. <mcreference link="https://dribbble.com/shots/25961695--Quit-Now-Smoking-Cessation-App" index="0">0</mcreference>

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Most smokers fail to quit due to lack of real-time progress visibility, structured reinforcement, and motivational rewards. There’s a need for an intuitive digital solution that provides both emotional and logical support — tracking health benefits, financial savings, and habit milestones. <mcreference link="https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/web-design" index="2">2</mcreference>

## 🎯 Goals

* Facilitate long-term smoking cessation using behavior-first design.
* Visualize tangible benefits like money saved, health gains, and increased life expectancy.
* Encourage daily commitment through challenges and rewards.
* Offer a supportive, non-judgmental environment to reduce relapse anxiety. <mcreference link="https://dribbble.com/shots/popular" index="1">1</mcreference>
